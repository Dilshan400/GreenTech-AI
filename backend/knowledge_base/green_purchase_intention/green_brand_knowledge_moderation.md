# Factors Influencing Green Purchase Intention: The Moderating Role of Green Brand Knowledge

**Authors**: Saeed Siyal, Munawar Javed Ahmed, Riaz Ahmad, Bushra Shahzad Khan, & Chunlin Xin  
**Publication**: *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health (IJERPH)*, Vol. 18, Issue 20, 2021, Article 10762.

---

### Conceptual Overview: Beyond the Theory of Reasoned Action
While the classic **Theory of Reasoned Action (TRA)** assumes that voluntary intentions alone predict consumer buying, non-volitional cognitive resources—specifically **Green Brand Knowledge (GBK)**—act as vital facilitators or bottlenecks in purchasing eco-friendly products.

---

### Tested Variables & Direct Structural Model
Evaluated through Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) with 396 consumers:
* **Green Brand Positioning (GBP)**: How effectively a brand communicates ecological values and green differentiation relative to competitors.
* **Attitude Toward Green Brands (ATGB)**: Consumers' overall favorable evaluation and trust in green product performance.
* **Environmental Concern (EC)**: Individual awareness of and emotional sensitivity toward environmental degradation.
* **Green Purchase Intention (GPI)**: Readiness to buy environmentally friendly products.

#### Direct Effects Results:
* **ATGB $\rightarrow$ GPI**: Significant positive effect ($\beta = 0.346, t = 6.612, p = 0.000$).
* **GBP $\rightarrow$ GPI**: Significant positive effect ($\beta = 0.323, t = 6.492, p = 0.000$).
* **EC $\rightarrow$ GPI**: Significant positive effect ($\beta = 0.223, t = 3.425, p = 0.001$).
* Total variance explained: **$R^2 = 0.506$** ($50.6\%$).

---

### Moderating Effects of Green Brand Knowledge (GBK)
Using the product-indicator moderation approach:
1. **ATGB $\times$ GBK $\rightarrow$ GPI**: **Statistically significant positive moderation** ($\beta = 0.125, t = 2.408, p = 0.016$).
   - *Interpretation*: The positive link between a consumer's favorable attitude and their actual intention to buy is substantially stronger when they possess in-depth green brand knowledge.
2. **EC $\times$ GBK $\rightarrow$ GPI**: **Statistically significant positive moderation** ($\beta = 0.119, t = 2.349, p = 0.019$).
   - *Interpretation*: Environmental concern alone is insufficient; when coupled with high green brand knowledge, consumers convert general ecological worry into active purchasing choices.
3. **GBP $\times$ GBK $\rightarrow$ GPI**: Insignificant moderation ($\beta = -0.073, p = 0.119$). Green brand positioning independently drives purchase intentions regardless of brand knowledge levels.

---

### Key Takeaway for Sustainable Product Recommendation
To translate eco-attitudes into real purchasing decisions, advisory platforms must provide **actionable brand knowledge**—explaining what specific eco-features a brand offers and verifying sustainability claims to empower consumers' buying decisions.
