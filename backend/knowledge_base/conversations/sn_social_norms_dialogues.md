# Social Norms and Peer Influence (SN) - Expert Q&A Knowledge

This knowledge reference document contains verified consultation dialogues and recommended answers for consumer and student inquiries regarding social influence.

### Question: Can my friends influence my decision to buy green electronics?
**Keywords**: friends,social influence,green electronics  
**Guidance Answer**: Yes. People often consider opinions from friends, classmates, family members, and other groups when making purchasing decisions. Their influence can affect how attractive or socially acceptable green purchasing seems.

### Question: Do university students influence each other to buy eco-friendly devices?
**Keywords**: students,peer influence,university  
**Guidance Answer**: They can. Discussions, recommendations, and shared experiences among students may influence perceptions of environmentally responsible purchasing. The strength of that influence differs between individuals.

### Question: Does my family matter when choosing an environmentally friendly laptop?
**Keywords**: family,social influence,laptop  
**Guidance Answer**: Family opinions can be one source of social influence, particularly for expensive purchases. You can consider their views while also evaluating your own needs, budget, and reliable product information.

### Question: Why do people care what others think about green purchases?
**Keywords**: social norms,green purchasing,approval  
**Guidance Answer**: Purchasing decisions can have a social component. People may value approval from important groups or feel encouraged when environmentally responsible behavior is common among people around them.

### Question: Can lecturers encourage students to buy sustainable electronics?
**Keywords**: lecturers,university,sustainability  
**Guidance Answer**: Lecturers and universities can provide information and create awareness about sustainable consumption. Their influence may be especially relevant when students are learning about technology and environmental issues.

### Question: Can university policies encourage green purchasing?
**Keywords**: university policy,green purchasing,e-waste  
**Guidance Answer**: Yes. Policies such as responsible e-waste collection, sustainability awareness programs, and guidance on energy-efficient equipment can create an environment that supports responsible purchasing and use.

### Question: If my friends prefer expensive green products, should I buy them too?
**Keywords**: friends,budget,green products  
**Guidance Answer**: Not necessarily. Social influence can affect preferences, but your decision should also consider your needs, budget, product evidence, and environmental characteristics.

### Question: Does seeing other students recycle electronics influence purchasing?
**Keywords**: recycling,students,social norms  
**Guidance Answer**: It can contribute to a social environment where responsible electronic consumption is more visible and accepted. However, individual decisions are influenced by multiple factors.

### Question: How can students promote green purchasing among friends?
**Keywords**: students,promotion,green purchasing  
**Guidance Answer**: Students can share reliable information, discuss product durability and energy efficiency, encourage repair and reuse, and share responsible e-waste disposal options without pressuring others.

### Question: Are social norms important for green purchase intention?
**Keywords**: social norms,purchase intention,TPB  
**Guidance Answer**: Social norms can be relevant because people may consider whether important others approve of or practice a behavior. Their influence can vary depending on the person and situation.

### Question: Would a student club help promote green electronics?
**Keywords**: student club,awareness,sustainability  
**Guidance Answer**: A student club could organize awareness activities, product-information sessions, repair or reuse initiatives, and responsible e-waste collection. These activities may make sustainable practices more visible.

### Question: Should I follow a friend's recommendation for a green laptop?
**Keywords**: friend recommendation,laptop,decision  
**Guidance Answer**: A friend's experience can be useful, but it should not be the only basis for your decision. Check independent information, specifications, warranty, repairability, energy efficiency, and suitability for your work.

### Question: Can social media affect green purchasing decisions?
**Keywords**: social media,green claims,verification  
**Guidance Answer**: Yes. Social media can expose users to product recommendations, environmental claims, peer opinions, and discussions. It is important to verify claims using reliable sources before purchasing.

### Question: Do classmates' opinions matter when buying electronic devices?
**Keywords**: classmates,electronics,opinions  
**Guidance Answer**: They may matter, especially when students share experiences about brands, durability, repairs, and value. However, personal requirements and credible product information should also be considered.

### Question: How can universities create positive social norms for green purchasing?
**Keywords**: universities,social norms,sustainability  
**Guidance Answer**: Universities can provide sustainability education, promote repair and reuse, make e-waste collection accessible, and publicly communicate responsible purchasing practices.
